AI Skills Should Be Free for Everyone

AI is changing how we work, create, and connect — but the people who could benefit most are often the last to get access. That's why every workshop is completely free: supported by a community grant, with no cost, no obligation, and no sales pitch. The only requirement is curiosity.

Pleasure

Bring back family memories — restore old photos, write a children's story for grandkids, plan a dream trip, or try AI art just for fun.

  • Photo restoration & memory keeping
  • Creative writing & storytelling
  • Travel, recipes & hobby projects

Productivity

Turn AI into a personal assistant — draft emails and letters in minutes, summarize long documents, organize notes, and save hours every single week.

  • Email, writing & document help
  • Research & summaries
  • Notes, memory & daily routines

Profit

Discover how AI can open doors — from study help and career skills to starting a side hustle — with hands-on projects that build real confidence.

  • Study & career skills
  • Side hustle & creative projects
  • Portfolio pieces you can keep

Who It's For

Two groups who deserve free access to the AI age — no barriers, no cost, no judgment.

01

Seniors

Stay curious and connected. Gentle, patient instruction with safety at the center — spot AI scams, protect your info, and enrich everyday life.

02

College Students

Get ahead with AI skills for studying, research, and your career. Build real projects without letting AI do the work for you.

03

Together

When seniors and students learn side-by-side, everyone wins. Intergenerational cohorts build bonds, beat isolation, and make the whole neighborhood more confident.

How the Program Runs

Small groups, a steady rhythm, and support between sessions

Foundations

What AI is, how to stay safe, and how to ask good questions — the same basics that unlock everything else.

The Three P's

Weekly deep-dives into Pleasure, Productivity, and Profit — live, hands-on practice with real projects you care about.

Your Toolkit

Build your personal AI setup — ChatGPT, image generation, voice, automation — and learn the workflows that fit your life.

Capstone

Present your own AI Life Project — and leave with a personal playbook, a troubleshooting cheat sheet, and a certificate.

6 weeks · 90-minute sessions · 4–8 people per group · In-person in North York or online · Support between sessions included · Completely free
