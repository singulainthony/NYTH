Step-by-Step Guide to Handling Suspicious Tech Support Calls

Follow these simple steps if you receive a suspicious call:

  1. Don't panic—scammers use urgency to cloud judgment; take a deep breath.
  2. Ask for the caller's name, company, and contact info, but don't trust it.
  3. Hang up immediately and search the company's official website for support.
  4. Call the verified number yourself to check if there's an issue.
  5. Never share personal info, screen access, or pay via wire/gift cards.
  6. Report the call to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre at 1-888-495-8501.
  7. Install call-blocking apps on your phone for future prevention.
