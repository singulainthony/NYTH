Step-by-Step Guide to Connecting Your Smart TV to the Internet

Here's how for Wi-Fi setup:

  1. Turn on your TV and go to settings menu.
  2. Select 'Network' or 'Internet' option.
  3. Choose Wi-Fi and scan for networks.
  4. Select your home network and enter password.
  5. Confirm connection; test with an app.
  6. For Ethernet, plug cable into TV and router.
  7. Restart if issues arise.
  8. Update TV software for best performance.
