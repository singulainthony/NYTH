Step-by-Step Guide to Organizing Your Email Inbox

Follow these steps for a cleaner inbox using Gmail as an example:

  1. Log in to your email and select all unread messages to review.
  2. Create folders/labels: Go to settings and add categories like 'Bills' or 'Personal.'
  3. Archive or delete old emails: Keep only recent ones in inbox.
  4. Set up filters: For new emails, create rules to auto-move to folders.
  5. Unsubscribe from unwanted newsletters via the email's link.
  6. Star important emails for quick access.
  7. Schedule weekly cleanups to maintain order.
  8. Sync with your phone for mobile management.
